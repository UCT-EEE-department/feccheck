# UCT EEE Curriculum Specifications, Credits, Equivalencies & Prerequisites

Source of truth: [program_defs.py](../../../../program_defs.py) and [fec_check.py](../../../../fec_check.py).

## 1. Programme Codes & Credit Targets
- **Electrical Engineering (`EE`)**: `EB009` (Standard, `N = 4`) / `EB809` (ASPECT, `N = 5`)
- **Mechatronics (`ME`)**: `EB011` (Standard, `N = 4`) / `EB811` (ASPECT, `N = 5`)
- **Electrical & Computer Engineering (`EC`)**: `EB022` (Standard, `N = 4`) / `EB822` (ASPECT, `N = 5`)
- **Minimum EEE Credits to Graduate (`rcurcr`)**:
  - `yfdreg >= 2025`: **560 credits**
  - `yfdreg < 2025`: **576 credits**
  - Only credits earned during years registered in an EEE programme (`EB009`, `EB809`, `EB011`, `EB811`, `EB022`, `EB822`) count toward `totalcr`.
